How it all began

Our Love Story

❦

It was a crisp autumn afternoon in 2019 when our paths first crossed at a small bookshop in the heart of the city. Emma was reaching for the last copy of a worn poetry collection — and James, ever the gentleman, offered it to her with a smile that would change both their lives forever.

What began as a chance encounter blossomed into countless coffee dates, long walks through the park, and a shared love for sunsets, old records, and quiet Sunday mornings. Through adventures near and far, their hearts grew closer with every passing season.

On a starlit evening in Santorini, with the Aegean Sea whispering below, James got down on one knee. Through tears of joy, Emma said yes — and now, with the love of family and friends surrounding them, they invite you to witness the next beautiful chapter of their story.
